# Continuity and Recurrence

Read after interruptions or recurrence, and before pausing unresolved diagnosis.

## Continuity Gate

On resume, re-read the latest request, ledger, and applicable decision/finding
indexes. Recover unresolved items and next proof steps, including changed scope,
priority, environment, or risk acceptance. A completed subtask does not close
the whole report. If storage is session-scoped, name its durability limit and
reconstruction source.

Before stopping unresolved diagnosis, preserve the works/fails matrix, ruled-out
hypotheses with disproving observations, environment epoch and control cases,
and one next decisive probe. On resume rerun working and failing controls before
trusting that discriminator. If both pass, record environment drift or
`not-reproduced`; the old matrix is historical evidence, and any robustness
change is a mitigation, not a proven root-cause fix.

## Recurrence Gate

When a bug class occurs at least twice, name the shared contract, scan adjacent
surfaces, and choose the most likely missed neighbor. Add a disconfirming check
or record why that neighbor is outside scope; do not broaden into unrelated
hardening.

When a symptom returns after a verified fix, rerun the prior layer's discriminator.
Reopen it only if it regressed; otherwise preserve it and open a new cause layer.
Recordings are strong evidence for visible sequence and periodicity; inferred delay or
magnitude remains a hypothesis until instrumented.
